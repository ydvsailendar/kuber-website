About Us

A Community-First Co-operative

Shree Kuber Saving & Credit Co-operative Ltd. was founded in 2071 BS to bring reliable and transparent financial services to the communities of Nawalparasi. Operating under Nepal's Co-operative Act and affiliated with NEFSCUN, we are fully owned and governed by our members.

From our head office in Bhujahawa to service centres in Bairahawa and Kudiya, we serve hundreds of families with savings accounts, loan facilities, and remittance services.
